How drivers are calculated

  • New — client present this month, absent last month (all current revenue).
  • Lost — client present last month, absent this month (−prior revenue).
  • Certs — same client + product: (Δ quantity) × prior rate.
  • Price — same client + product: current quantity × (Δ rate).
  • Benefits — product lines added or dropped for an existing client.
  • Other — lines missing quantity/rate, or amount residual vs qty×rate.

Excludes Referral Fees:Email Feed and Statement of Work (not client revenue).
