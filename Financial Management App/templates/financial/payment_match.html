Match Invoices & Payments

Batch-apply unapplied QuickBooks payments to open invoices using a prepared Accounts Receivable aging export.

Prepare the Excel file

Start from the regular Accounts receivable aging summary export in QuickBooks. Clean it up before upload — the automation expects two columns, either Customer + CURRENT or Account + Payment.

  1. Download the A/R aging summary report.
  2. Delete header rows above the column titles and footer rows (totals, date lines).
  3. Filter the Total column for blanks; delete those rows.
  4. Delete customer subtotal rows (bold “Total” rows).
  5. Filter aging buckets 31–60, 61–90, and 90 and over — delete rows where any of those are not blank.
  6. Delete any row where the total is not zero.
  7. Clear customer-column indents (Format cells → indent: 0).
  8. Copy the first two columns to a new sheet; rename them Customer and CURRENT (or Account and Payment).

What the automation does

  • Opens QuickBooks Online in Edge (you log in manually).
  • For each customer in the sheet: search → open → Receive payment on an invoice.
  • Reads the payment date from the first unapplied payment or deposit credit.
  • Verifies Deposit To is ATB - Trust.
  • Sets the payment date and confirms amount received is $0.00 before saving.
