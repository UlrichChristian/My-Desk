## Session — My Desk / ASO cross-login bug

**Symptom:** logging into My Desk (:5002) logged you out of the ASO app (:5000), and vice versa.

**Cause:** browser cookies are scoped by *host*, not host+port. `127.0.0.1:5002` and
`127.0.0.1:5000` share one cookie jar. Both apps used Flask's default cookie name
`session` but different signing keys (`C_APP_SECRET_KEY` vs `ASO_SECRET_KEY`), so each
app overwrote the other's cookie and then failed the signature check — Flask silently
discards an unverifiable session cookie and shows the login page.

**Fix:** set `SESSION_COOKIE_NAME = "cdesk_session"` in `c.py` `create_app()`.
Only one side needs a distinct name; ASO keeps `session`.

**Open item:** `c.py:38` falls back to `os.urandom(32)` when `C_APP_SECRET_KEY` is unset,
so My Desk mints a new signing key on every restart and logs you out. There is no `.env`
in `C:/Christian` and nothing loads dotenv. ASO has a `.env` with `ASO_SECRET_KEY`, which
is why only My Desk lost sessions on restart. Proposed fix: persist a generated key to a
gitignored file on first run. Not implemented — awaiting decision.
