## 21:34 � CMRRA commission base deep-dive (with Claude)
Extended analytical session investigating how Manulife calculates the commission line on CMRRA's ASO invoices. Pulled 13 months of statements (Oct 2024 � Apr 2026) plus reconciliation screenshots for Jan�Apr 2026.

**What we set out to answer:** Is commission 5% of benefits paid, 5% of some subset of admin fee bases, or something else entirely?

**Process:**
- Cross-referenced commission amounts against every visible invoice line: total benefits paid, dental/health admin bases, HCSA bases (dental and health separately), pooling charges, risk charges, Ontario RST, premium tax. Tested all permutations and combinations.
- Discovered and confirmed the 2-month billing lag: commission on Month N invoice = activity from Month N-2. Confirmed via March 2026 invoice showing exactly $922.56 = January 2026 recon earnings.
- Confirmed internal invoice identity: dental_benefits = dental_admin_base + HCSA_dental; health_benefits = health_admin_base + HCSA_health ? health_pooling. Holds to the cent across all 13 months.
- Tested all formulas across 13 months. Implied rate ranged 4.07%�6.92% � no consistent claims-based formula. Nov 2025 was the clearest anomaly: $976.33 commission vs only $14,100 in benefits ($5,427 above what any formula would produce).

**Conclusion:** Commission base is a Manulife-internal premium equivalent (enrolled lives � rate table), set at renewal and not visible on the ASO statement. Rate is confirmed 5%; base is opaque. To audit: request "premium equivalent base for policies 112479 and 128564" from Manulife directly.

**Annual trend:** Rolling 3-month avg base rose from ~$15,800 (late 2024) to ~$20,700 (early 2026) � +16.5% YoY, consistent with annual renewal rate adjustment. Nov 2025 flagged as possible catch-up month worth confirming with Manulife.
