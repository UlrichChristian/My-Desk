## 10:58 — Cascadia/NAP Steel RBC reconciliation — status quo approach
**Cascadia Metals / NAP Steel – RBC (Manulife... no, RBC) billing reconciliation note**

- Cascadia GSI Division and NAP Steel are affiliated entities under the same RBC billing relationship.
- Both Cascadia and NAP Steel are currently showing a **credit balance** with RBC that we have been unable to reconcile (source of the credit is unclear — possibly related to the Policy Change - GSI adjustments seen flowing through the Cascadia statements Nov 2024–May 2025, but not confirmed).
- Given we can't trace the discrepancy back to its origin, decided to reframe the reconciliation goal: instead of chasing the historical variance to zero, treat this as a **status quo (point-in-time) reconciliation**.
  - Goal: ensure *today's* records align between EA and RBC on a membership basis, not a dollar-history basis.
  - Any member currently insured must be listed as insured with **both** EA and RBC.
  - Any member not currently insured must **not** appear as insured under either EA or RBC.
- This membership-alignment approach is considered easier to execute than fully explaining the legacy credit discrepancy, and should be the working approach going forward for Cascadia/NAP Steel until/unless the credit source is identified.

## 15:45 — Carrier Billing Reconciliation tool built (Financial app)
- New tile in Financial Management (`/financial/carrier-recon`): upload RBC billing
  report + EA statement, get a member-by-member reconciliation in the browser.
- Reads RBC `Billing Details` tab (Premium + Adj's, before tax) and EA
  `Premium by Benefit` tab filtered to a benefit line (default `Individual LTD`).
- Matches on EA Cert = RBC Alternate Id, ignoring leading zeros (RBC pads
  inconsistently — `000108` vs `59`).
- Sort: one-sided members first, then variances (largest first), then clean matches.
- Files: `Financial Management App/fin_services/carrier_recon.py`,
  `fin_web/routes.py`, `templates/financial/carrier_recon.html`, `financial.css`.
- **Key discovery (Canada Pet Health, Sep 2025):** for legacy members with short
  IDs, RBC Alternate Id and EA Cert are *different numbering systems* — e.g. ID
  `24` is MACKIE at RBC but WINGENBACH at EA. This explains most of the 11 RBC-only
  / 12 EA-only members and several of the 41 "variances". The tool flags these by
  showing the EA name in red under the RBC name when a matched cert's last names
  disagree. The $449.21 total variance ($3,449.95 RBC vs $3,000.74 EA) is a mix of
  genuine premium differences and these crossed IDs.
- Small infra tweak: `c.py` now falls back to the `PORT` env var (after `C_APP_PORT`)
  and `.claude/launch.json` has `autoPort: true`, so a preview instance can run
  alongside the live :5002 server.
