# Carriers
> Carrier contacts, portals, and remittance quirks (Canada Life, GSC, Manulife, etc.).


## Carrier Code Disambiguation

- **MABC** = Manitoba Blue Cross
- **MBC** = Medavie Blue Cross

These are easily confused — always confirm which one before creating tasks, PnP notes, or referencing "MBC/MABC" in correspondence.

## Reconciliation App Scope

The reconciliation app (ReconciliationClaudeLink) only covers a select group of carriers that support **self-funded (ASO) plans** — this is why some carriers don't show up in that system.

**AIG, RBC, and iASM are purely insured-premium carriers** — out of scope for the reconciliation app by design, not a setup gap. Their remittance advices (e.g. the August revised-system batch) are handled separately from the ASO reconciliation workflow.
