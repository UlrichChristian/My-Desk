## Preferred Share Redemption

**Class D Preferred Shares — Redemption Formula**
- Par value per share: $0.0000354
- Total redemption value per share: $1.00

**How to calculate the split each month:**
1. Get the number of shares being redeemed
2. Share Capital = shares × $0.0000354 (round to 2 decimals)
3. Retained Earnings = total redemption value − Share Capital
4. Total redemption = shares × $1.00

**Journal Entry:**
- Dr. Retained Earnings — redemption amount
- Dr. Share Capital — par value amount
- Cr. Liability/Cash — total redemption value
